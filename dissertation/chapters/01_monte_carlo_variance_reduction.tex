\section{Risk-Neutral Valuation}
Under risk-neutral pricing, the time-0 option value is the discounted expectation of payoff under the risk-neutral measure.

\section{Standard Monte Carlo}
A standard Monte Carlo estimator simulates independent paths, evaluates discounted payoffs, and averages across samples.

\section{Convergence and Standard Error}
The estimator converges at rate $\mathcal{O}(N^{-1/2})$, with standard error estimated via sample variance.

\section{Antithetic Variates}
Antithetic pairing introduces negatively correlated samples by reusing Gaussian draws with opposite signs to reduce variance.

\section{Control Variates}
Control variates exploit covariance with a known-expectation variable to reduce estimator variance through an optimally chosen coefficient.

\section{Arithmetic and Geometric Asian Options}
For Asian options, the geometric-average counterpart is analytically tractable in Black--Scholes and provides a natural classical control for the arithmetic-average payoff.

\section{Dimensionality and Limitations of Classical Controls}
Classical controls are effective when strong correlations and tractable moments are available, but performance can deteriorate in high-dimensional or misspecified settings.

\section{Neural and Automatic Control Variates}
Neural/automatic control variates parameterise control families and fit them from data, aiming to capture structure beyond hand-crafted controls while preserving unbiasedness constraints.
